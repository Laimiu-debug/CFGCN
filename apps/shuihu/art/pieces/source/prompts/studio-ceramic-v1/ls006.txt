One full-body Water Margin glazed stoneware figurine, natural six-head adult proportions, hand-modelled ceramic sculpture. Warm ivory unglazed clay face and hands; coloured vitreous glaze on garments, thicker and darker in folds, soft continuous reflections, small exposed clay foot rims. Armour and weapons are sculpted fired clay, not metal. Lin Chong, lean broad-shouldered martial instructor, stern eyes and fine moustache; folded cloth cap, layered robe and lamellar vest. Deep indigo robe glaze, celadon panels, small iron-red sash, ivory raised armour relief. Steady stance, one hand holding one upright spear: leaf tip at the top, straight shaft ending in a rounded clay butt beside his boot. Finished back, hands and feet. Static unrigged. No pedestal, scene, text, candy, resin, metallic gold, chibi head or loose parts.
